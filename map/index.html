Line Down: when the subway stops, does Toronto grab a bike?

Real TTC delays, replayed. Bike Share stations near the stop light up when more trips start there than on a normal day.

stopped station (dashed ring: 1 km) more bike trips than normal about normal fewer than normal no trips either day

Normal day = the same weekday and time, 1-4 weeks before and after, with no subway delay on that line. One disruption is noisy: weather and events move these numbers too. Averaged over 3,777 delays of 10+ minutes, trips within 250 m rise 13.6% in the first hour (95% range 9.9% to 17.3%), which is less than one extra trip per delay. Data: TTC delay log, Bike Share Toronto trips, TTC GTFS.
